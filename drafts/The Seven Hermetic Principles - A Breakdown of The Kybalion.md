> [!abstract] The Secret of Science
> [[Isaac Newton|Sir Isaac Newton]], [[Johannes Kepler]], and [[Francis Bacon]] were pioneering figures in the development of modern science. Yet, they were only deciphering the world using an ancient set of tools kept secret for centuries: the Hermetica. 

The Hermetica is an ancient Egyptian philosophy distilling wisdom on the core essences of reality. Followers of the Hermetica have used its secrets to shape human understanding through new technological and philosophical discoveries. For millennia, these teachings have been kept alive by dedicated followers through secret practices into modern times. By the early 1900s, a book called _[[The Kybalion]]_ was published, breaking down the core seven principles and other axioms of [[Hermeticism]]. Using this as a guide, we shall learn how to apply the seven principles to everyday life.

## The Principle of Mentalism

> "THE ALL IS MIND; The Universe is Mental."

THE ALL serves as the foundational source of reality and the mental container in which the universe exists. Much like an author crafting an intricate narrative, creation occurs not by assembling physical materials, but through mental projection. Though the characters inside a story operate with their own internal agency, they remain woven from the author's consciousness. In this same way, a fragment of the ultimate creator remains within us, and our experiences, in turn, feed back into the evolving consciousness of THE ALL.

If THE ALL constructs the universe through mind, then human beings, made in that same image, construct their immediate personal reality through their own minds. We do not just react to an objective world; we actively author the meaning of that world. Mastering Mentalism isn't about magical thinking; it is the application of conscious narrative design.

---
## The Principle of Correspondence

> "As above, so below; as below, so above."

The world is made of different planes of existence. The most distinct ways for these planes to be sorted is into the Physical Plane, Mental Plane, and Spiritual Plane. The Physical Plane correspondences with matter and energy, the Mental Plane with the minds of living things (such as plants, animals, and humans), and the Spiritual Plane with forms of being higher than man (often dealing with angels and gods).

Far from being isolated realms, the planes share a fundamental connection originating from a single source: THE ALL. An electron orbits a nucleus as a planet orbits a star. This structural symmetry is what makes 'As above, so below; as below, so above' a tool for discovery: by understanding the observable laws of the physical world, we gain a map to navigate the unseen laws of the mental and spiritual worlds.

## The Principle of Vibration

> _"Nothing rests; everything moves; everything vibrates."_

Everything is made of vibrations. THE ALL creates its mental images as a series of vibrations. The lower planes are but a low degree of vibration that they appear as solid matter while higher vibrations unseeable. At the densest end of the spectrum, slow vibrations manifest as seemingly solid matter. At the highest end, vibration becomes so intensely rapid that it appears completely stationary—much like a wheel spinning so fast that its spokes blur into a single, motionless image. Because all thoughts, emotions, and physical objects are simply different frequencies of the same ultimate reality, we gain agency: by consciously shifting our internal frequency, we can alter our moods, influence our surrounding environment, and transmute our state of being.

---
## The Principle of Polarity

> _"Everything is Dual; everything has poles; everything has its pair of opposites..."_

If everything is made of vibrations, things are only different in how they vibrate. Because all phenomena are built on vibration, apparent opposites are actually identical in nature—differing only in degree. Heat and cold are not two separate forces, but two ends of a single continuum called temperature. Light and dark are merely varying degrees of illumination. Applying this to the mental plane reveals that states like Fear and Courage, or Love and Hate, exist on identical spectrums. Mastery comes not from attempting to destroy a negative state, but from 'Mental Transmutation'—shifting your internal frequency up or down the spectrum toward your desired pole.

## The Principle of Rhythm

> _"Everything flows, out and in; everything has its tides; all things rise and fall..."_

In a world of constant vibrations which collide together, there is constant adjustment and movement. This allows over time for a vibration on a pole to swing from one end of the pole to the other, much like a pendulum swings in equal motion left and right. Although this natural law of reality does not always swing from the extreme poles, it does move back and forth. This is how during a day we swing from being awake and energized to tired and sleepy then back the next day.

Now the swinging of pendulum occurs on the lower planes of consciousness and matter, not affecting the upper conscious planes. Thus it is possible to use the force of the lower plane to move you to where you want to be and then using the upper consciousness stay in the spot you desire to be. This is called the Law of Neutralization. An example of such using your body's natural Circadian Rhythm to wake you up and gain your morning energy and then as your body gets tired to use your conscious mind to hold that energy for longer.

## The Principle of Cause and Effect

> _"Every Cause has its Effect; every Effect has its Cause..."_

Cause and Effect is a concept many are familiar with but don't truly understand. It seems simple to think that when one thing happens that something else might occur. Yet, it is infinitely more complex than that. Most things occur not just by a single thing but many simultaneous things, making it hard to see the underlying causes.

When people say that things happen by "luck" what they really mean is they do not understand the causes to have an effect happen. Such event might be playing poker. A novice will think while there is some strategy to the game, there is luck in getting cards. Meanwhile a professional player will know exactly the way cards are shuffled, the amount of cards in the deck, and how they are dealt, allowing them to use this information to play at a higher level. There is no luck, there is only unseen causes.

Ultimately, knowing the causes of things will always lead to understanding how to achieve an effect. So true Hermetic Masters attempt to understand the root causes so they control the effects to their personal desires. They then become causers in themselves instead of just the product of effects.

## The Principle of Gender

> _"Gender is in everything; everything has its Masculine and Feminine Principles..."_

The concepts of masculine and feminine have been greatly understood by the vast amounts of cultures. Masculine and Feminine through the lower planes are just the physical body parts on living beings, but yet there is also the higher planes. The Feminine is passive, chaotic, creative ability that pushes boundaries around it. Meanwhile the Masculine is active, intentional order that seeks to set boundaries and place items inside those boundaries. Both of these aspects have their positive and negative sides. However the best of both is achieved when the Masculine directs energy into the Feminine allowing it to do creative work as they are complementary forces.

Everything has a both Masculine and Feminine Genders. In terms of a person, we all have emotions, ideas, and desires as our passive, receiving Feminine nature which is what is called the "Me". Meanwhile there is conscious and active thought that guides the energy called the "I" which directs energy into the "Me". Most people very rarely use the "I" to direct their energy instead allowing outside forces to direct the energy of the "Me" into their own desires. Thus we must use the Eye of Perception to look at how our internal "I" and "Me" are operating to establish proper order within ourselves.

## Synthesis

These Hermetic Principles have been the core ideas for many thinkers and ideas through the course of time. 

- **[[Isaac Newton]]**: Newton's Third Law of Motion states "for every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction". This is not only stated like the principles above, but is based in the Principles of Cause and Effect where you need the causer to create the effect. Applying the Principle of Correspondence we can see that the 3rd Law of Motion applies on not only everyday objects but to the extremes of tiniest atoms to the galactic planetary bodies.
- [[Carl Jung]]
	- **Persona & Shadow**: A persona is the person's outwards appearance shown to the world while his shadow is the subconscious inner core. The persona and shadow act as two poles, aligning with the Principle of Polarity. 
	- **Animus & Anima**: Jung's Animus and Anima, corresponds to the masculine "I" and feminine "me" within the Principle of Gender.

By using the Law of Neutralization, one can better aim upwards while being saved from falling. And above all, he has the keys to unlock new potential in himself and his environment.
