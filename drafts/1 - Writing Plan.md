### Layer 1: No Focus Topic

Use this layer when you are between deep dives, short on time, or simply want to follow immediate curiosity across any of your five domains.

### 🟢 DO
- **Follow immediate friction/interest:** Write about whatever is on your mind right now, regardless of discipline.
- **Mine the stubs:** Look at uncreated files (`[[Ghost Links]]`) and existing atomic notes to see what is ripe for a standalone article.
- **Seed the garden with Atom Notes:** When you encounter a compelling fact, chess line, anecdote, or software pattern, immediately create a note where the *title* is the idea, and drop a few relevant internal links inside. 

### 🔴 DO NOT
- **DO NOT** worry about cohesive themes or macro-level narrative arcs in this phase.
- **DO NOT** leave interesting facts or ideas trapped in your head—always offload them into an Atom note name with a couple of quick links.

---

## Layer 2: Focus Topic

Use this layer when a specific concept or cluster of Atom notes gains enough gravity that you want to dedicate 1–3 months to expanding it systematically.

### 🟢 DO
- **Create a Focus Hub Page:** Create a dedicated note for the overarching topic and link every individual article idea you want to write under it.
- **Build from the bottom up:** Start by writing the smaller, hyper-specific topic notes linked to that Focus Hub.
- **Weave the web:** As you write these smaller pieces, actively look for opportunities to link them *to each other*, not just back to the parent hub.
- **Keep seeding Layer 1:** Continue dropping random, unrelated Atom notes into your vault as you get them so other domains passively grow while you focus.
- **Execute the Capstone Exit:** After 1–3 months (when ideas for the focus topic naturally thin out), write the **Main Focus Note** that binds all your written smaller pieces together. 
- **Clean up the debris:** Before closing the Focus Topic, convert any remaining unwritten notes on the hub page into permanent Layer 1 Atom notes for future use.

### 🔴 DO NOT
- **DO NOT** lose momentum trying to write the massive Main Focus Note first. Build the foundation with small pieces first.
- **DO NOT** abandon your passive note-taking in other domains just because you are focused on one topic.

---

## Layer 3: Bridges

Use this layer upon wrapping up a Focus Topic to create high-leverage, multidisciplinary synthesis essays that prevent your domains from feeling isolated.

### 🟢 DO
- **Audit your finished Focus Topics:** Look back at your newly completed Focus Topic and actively map lines of sight to *other* distinct Focus Topics in your vault.
- **Aim for Multi-Point Collisions:** Try to force connections between **more than two** distinct disciplines at once (e.g., Chess Theory + Anthropology + Game Architecture) to generate truly unique, long-form academic deep dives.

### 🔴 DO NOT
- **DO NOT** force a bridge if the supporting groundwork isn't there yet. If a connection feels hollow, leave it as a Layer 1 Atom note until your vault has more pages to support it.
