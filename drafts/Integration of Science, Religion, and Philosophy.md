In the articles *[[What is Science]]?*, *[[What is Religion]]?*, and *[[What is Philosophy]]?* the distinction between these three pillars of thought have been laid out. While there are a lot of differences between the three, often causing clashes between each pillar, there is a way to integrate them together into one unison world view. We do this by laying out each ones strengths and weakness and then picking the best tool for the job.


| Pillar of Thught | Strengthes                                                                                              | Weakness                                                                                                 |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Philosophy       | Creating logical arguments to explain the world around us.                                              | Can't explain thing we can't make arguments for or create logical arguments that don't effect reality.   |
| Science          | Understanding how the material world works through testing.                                             | Cannot explain why a thing is the way it is, it can just see that something does behave the way it does. |
| Relgion          | Creating meaning and call of action into people lives.<br>Gives an orientation for personable actions.  | Symbiotic text can perceive as literal truths. <br>                                                      |







Examples of Integrations
St. Augustine tried to combine scientific thinking which religious spiritualism. 
Thomas Aquinas tried to integrate Aristotle's ideas with Christian religious thinking. 
Jordan Peterson taking Jungian Psychology integrating with religious texts and enlightenment thinking.


Integration Steps
1) Religion should be used as your spiritual world view and define how you engage in the world
2) Science should be used only as a testing method to define how the material world works.
3) Philosophy should be used as way to logically understand human nature and world.
4) Deal with conflicts
	- **Can it be measured or physically tested?** Science has the final word. (What is).
	- **Is it a question of ethics, logic, or structural human behavior?** Philosophy has the final word. (Why and How we think).
	- **Is it a question of personal meaning, purpose, or transcendent experience?** Religion has the final word. (What gives my life value?).


It is important to state that there some area where fields so fundamentally overlap that you have to make your best judgment on which area makes the most sense.
