# Great Man

> [!abstract] Definition 
> An individual — regardless of gender — of extraordinary historical footprint whose singular will, ideas, or actions reshape civilization on a grand scale, leaving a permanent legacy that alters the trajectory of the world.

### 🎯 Criteria
- **Core Requirement:** The individual's actions must single-handedly redirect or define an entire historical era, culture, political structure, or religious landscape with multi-generational, long-term consequences. 
- **Exclusions:** This term measures historical disruption and systemic impact; it explicitly excludes any metric of moral righteousness or "goodness." A Great Man can be profoundly destructive or morally reprehensible, as the designation relies solely on the scale of their footprint, not their virtue.

### ⚡ Examples
* [[Napoleon Bonaparte]] — Reshaped the geopolitical map of Europe and established legal frameworks that outlasted his empire.
* [[John Locke]] / [[Thomas Hobbes]] — Authored political treaties that fundamentally redefined human governance and catalyzed global revolutions.
* [[Jesus of Nazareth]] / [[Prophet Muhammad]] — Founded world religions that completely restructured global civilization, morality, and history for millennia.
* **[[Catherine the Great]] —** After seizing the Russian throne via a bloodless coup d'état against her own husband, she forcibly modernized and Westernized Russian, firmly embedding the empire into the theater of global affairs for the next two centuries.
* *Non-Example:* [[Marcus Aurelius]] — An exemplary moral figure and a brilliant defender of the Roman Empire. However, his life was spent _preserving_ an existing system against decay rather than radically shattering or restructuring the global order. He left the world structurally identical to how he found it.
