## ⚡ The Operating Framework: What Makes a Figure "Great"?

Within the context of *The Roman Forum*, the designation of a **Great Figure** relies on a strict, analytical framework rather than casual fame or moral celebration. To understand how these individuals are evaluated, cross-reference our core foundational definitions:

**[[Great Man|Great Men]]**: The operating definition requiring that an individual’s singular will, ideas, or actions reshape civilization on a grand scale, reshape world.
**Morality Exclusion**: This framework intentionally excludes "goodness". A Great Figure makes an outsized impact, sometimes in morally good or the morally bad.
