## Who is Aristotle?

Aristotle was an ancient Greek philosopher and polymath who stands as one of the most influential figures in Western history. As a student of [[Plato]] and tutor to [[Alexander the Great]], he established the Lyceum and pioneered the formal study of logic, biology, and ethics. His work serves as the primary bridge between ancient metaphysical inquiry and the structured logic that informs modern disciplines like Computer Science.

## Core Ideas

- **The Golden Mean:** The ethical concept that virtue exists as the balance between two extremes of deficiency and excess.
    
- **Syllogistic Logic:** The first formal system of reasoning, which laid the groundwork for deductive logic and the eventual binary structures used in modern algorithms.

## Notable Contributions

> [!abstract] Key Works & Milestones
> 
> - **[[Nicomachean Ethics]]:** A seminal text on virtue ethics that continues to influence modern Psychology and moral philosophy.
