This introduces a massive blind spot: it assumes the human ego is fully transparent to itself. ### 3. Areas of Expansion

Jungian archetypes, though less statistically "reliable" by modern scientific standards, attempt to engage with the symbolic, mythic, and unconscious dimensions of the human experience. Why has modern secular society abandoned the mythic depth of Jung for the sterile statistics of the Big 5?
- **The Nominalist Trap:** The Big 5 is based on the "lexical hypothesis"—the idea that all important individual differences have become encoded into language. This is a highly nominalist position (the belief that universals are merely names/words). You could introduce a realist counterargument: do these five traits represent objective, ontological structures of the human soul, or are they merely linguistic constructs we have agreed upon to organize social utility?
