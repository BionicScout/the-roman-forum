1) Define the topic. Take a topic you want to write on and create a clear goal for your post. Then create a Thesis for the topic. Ask AI to review your thesis.
2) Outline. In Essay.App, write out your thesis. Then break your thesis into different sections creating your outline. Ask AI to review your outline.
3) Drafting. Write the rough draft for each outlined section from memory with out looking things up if possible.
4) Rewrite. Rewrite each section to improve sentence structure and refine idea development. Ask AI to critique each section to find improvements.
5) Reorder. Move the order of the sentences to make the most sense.
6) Formatting. Go through the essay and format it for a blog style post. Include Headers, lists, and link syntax. Then copy essay over to obsidian to add extra callouts and metadata. Have AI check the formatting quality.
